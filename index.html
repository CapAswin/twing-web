How it works

From SMS to spending timeline, in three steps.

Everything happens on the phone in your pocket. Twing never sends a message, an amount, or a card number anywhere else.

01

Your bank texts you

The same card-payment alert your bank already sends for every purchase, refund, or top-up.

02

Twing reads and parses it, on-device

Merchant, amount, currency, and card are extracted locally. Personal messages and OTPs are filtered out and never stored.

03

It lands in your ledger

Categorized automatically, matched to a card, and ready to search, chart, or export — no manual typing.

Why SMS access, and why it's safe

Reading a text message isn't the same as reading your bank account.

Android only grants access to your SMS inbox as a whole — there's no bank-specific permission to ask for instead. Twing uses that access for one purpose: finding the card-payment alerts your bank already sends, and ignoring everything else.

Read the full privacy policy →
  • ✓ No internet permission in this build — there's no network path for your data to travel on.
  • ✓ Personal messages and one-time passwords are filtered out before anything is stored.
  • ✓ No cloud backup of app data — your ledger stays off Google's device-transfer and cloud backups.
  • ✓ You can turn off SMS scanning at any time and clear your local records from Settings.
  • ✓ No ad SDKs, no analytics SDKs, nothing tracking how you use the app.
